# 👾 Alien Radio

**Deep Space Transmissions · 12 Live Channels · GP Suite AI · Infinity Star Token Stage**

Alien Radio is a futuristic digital-radio platform with live internet streams, Web Audio fallback synthesizers, a spectrum analyzer, research tools, local prototype accounts and wallets, and a new Infinity token-creation layer.

## Working radio system

- **12 channels** backed by live internet radio streams with synthesized alien-sound fallback.
- **Spectrum analyzer** with bars, wave, and orbital visualization.
- **GP Suite AI dashboard** for Gitflow, Gitpulse, Gitarch, Gitsync, Gitscan, and Gitpin.
- **Research writer and library** for television and radio topics.
- **Signal Catch** demonstration game.
- **Local prototype wallet** for listening, game, and research records.
- **Responsive HUD interface** using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

## Infinity Star Token Stage

Open:

```text
stage.html
```

The enhanced route loads the working Alien Radio site and adds the universal Infinity conversion system.

### Universal ⭐ mark

Every convertible item receives the same subtle upper-right ⭐ mark:

- 15 × 15 pixels;
- quiet partial opacity until hover or keyboard focus;
- identical position and behavior;
- opens the selected item in the creation portal;
- means **create, edit, version, convert, or inspect ancestry**;
- does not mean favorite or rating.

Current marked objects include:

- Alien Radio itself;
- the active station;
- every channel;
- GP Suite AI modules;
- wallet and research products;
- created stations and Star Blueprints.

### Token types

#### Product Token

A typed production plan for the exact product or service Infinity needs. The planner scores:

- system demand;
- scarcity;
- human usefulness;
- production readiness;
- local capacity.

The static prototype calculates the minimum station supply plus a contingency reserve and shows the factors in canvas charts.

#### Star Blueprint ⭐

A named, versioned, attributable, reversible creation record. Each new save creates another version and preserves its parent rather than overwriting the earlier design.

#### Avatar Coin

A station-identity activation unit.

Prototype rule:

```text
Another verified user actively uses the creator's station design
for 24 continuous qualified hours
        ↓
One Avatar Coin is produced
        ↓
One Avatar Coin is locked to one active radio-station identity
```

Creator self-use, hidden tabs, stopped playback, bots, and disconnected use do not qualify. The page also includes a clearly labeled demonstration-hour control for testing the interface; demonstration coins cannot activate a production station.

## Prototype boundary

The wallet, Product Tokens, Star Blueprints, Avatar Coins, Bitcoin values, listening rewards, game rewards, and transaction feed are local software demonstrations. They do not create money, deposits, cryptocurrency, securities, guaranteed revenue, transferable legal ownership, or a production identity system.

A real implementation requires protected accounts, server-side verification, bot and fraud controls, signed station manifests, privacy review, rights management, payment and tax review, independent security testing, and auditable qualified-use records.
